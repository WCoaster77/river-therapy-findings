What's working well

These are worth keeping in any redesign.

  • ✓
    Clear pricing. Each trip shows prices for 1 to 5 people, with what's included and what isn't. Many charter sites don't do this.
  • ✓
    A strong offer. The 100% catch guarantee and the "you don't pay" promise are compelling and appear prominently.
  • ✓
    Useful planning content. The What to Expect page has seasonal packing lists, a fish calendar, launch maps and an FAQ. This answers most customer questions before they call.
  • ✓
    People-focused. Guide bios, real testimonials and the "fishing as therapy" message give the business a personal, trustworthy feel.
  • ✓
    Online booking through Peek works from every page.

Problems that may be costing you bookings

Listed most urgent first.

Your cabin page is broken

Urgent

The homepage "View Our Retreat" button leads to an error page (404). No other page on the site has cabin details, photos, rates or booking. Anyone interested in the cabin hits a dead end.

Two "Call" buttons dial the wrong number

Urgent
  • On the Trips & Rates page, the "Call 604-354-1967" button dials a number that is missing a digit.
  • On the Contact page, the phone link is also typed incorrectly.

The number in the footer works. Customers tapping the other buttons on their phones will not reach you.

Gift card buttons lead to an "out of stock" page

Urgent

Two buttons send customers to the gift card page:

  • Trips & Rates: the "Purchase a Gift Card" button at the bottom of the page.
  • What to Expect: the "Gift Card" link in the FAQ answer about gift certificates.

That page says "out of stock and unavailable." Only in its small print does it tell people to use Book Now instead. Most buyers will stop at "out of stock," which means lost sales, especially before Christmas.

Fix: point both buttons straight to the gift card option in your Peek booking page, so buyers land on gift cards and not on the general trip list. Retire or redirect the old out-of-stock page. We need the exact Peek gift card link from you.

No Terms & Conditions or Privacy Policy

Urgent

The footer links for both go nowhere. You take bookings and collect personal information through the contact form, so these pages should exist. Your cancellation and refund policy, and the exact terms of the catch guarantee, aren't stated anywhere I could find.

Information that is unclear or doesn't match

Customers may notice these and get confused or call to ask.

  • Trip lengths don't match between pages.The homepage lists 4hr, 8hr and Canyon trips. The Trips page lists 4hr, 6hr, 8hr, 10hr, Canyon and Wilderness.
  • Canyon trip.No trip length is shown, and the page doesn't say whether the catch guarantee applies.
  • Wilderness trip.The title says "Steelhead," but the description says fly fishing for bull trout. It's also unclear whether $1595 is per boat or per person, and how many people can go.
  • 10hr trip.The title says "Chilliwack or Agassiz," but the description says "Chilliwack, Mission or Agassiz." Agassiz isn't in your list of launch locations on the What to Expect page.
  • Booking buttons.The Full Day and 10hr trips use the same booking link. Please check that each one opens the correct trip in Peek.
  • "Book Your Trip" button.On the What to Expect page it goes to the Contact page, while every other page goes to online booking.
  • Taxes.The site doesn't say whether prices include tax.

Smaller fixes

  • The footer still says "Copyright © 2022."
  • Keenan's photo is labelled "Ken Chambers" behind the scenes. Search engines and screen readers see this label.
  • Typos: "life coats" should be "life jackets." "Lilooet" is spelled two ways; the correct spelling is "Lillooet."
  • Some address links on the What to Expect launch maps are broken.
  • The What to Expect page isn't in the top menu, so it's only reachable through buttons on other pages.
  • The fish calendar appears on two pages, so every update has to be made twice.
